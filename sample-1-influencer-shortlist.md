# SAMPLE — Influencer Research & Shortlist

*Sample work prepared to show my research method. Brand and creator details are examples; the process, checks and format are exactly what I deliver on a real project.*

## The brief (example)
- Brand: skincare startup launching a vitamin C serum
- Target audience: women 22–35, US/UK, interested in skincare routines
- Creator size: micro and mid-tier (10K–500K followers)
- Platforms: Instagram + TikTok
- Goal: gifted reviews first, paid posts for top performers

## How I research
1. Start from the audience, not the follower count — who actually watches this creator?
2. Pull creators from niche hashtags, competitor tags, and "similar accounts" chains.
3. Check every candidate by hand before they go on the list (see quality checks below).
4. Record contact details (email from bio / link page) so outreach can start immediately.

## Quality checks I apply to every creator
- Engagement quality: real comments from real accounts, not pods or one-word spam
- Consistency: posting regularly in recent weeks
- Audience fit: content language, location signals, and commenter profiles match the target
- Brand safety: no controversial or off-brand content in recent posts
- Past brand work: how they handled previous sponsored posts
