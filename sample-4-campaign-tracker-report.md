## What worked
- Honest review formats (first impressions, before/after) converted far better than routine mentions
- Creators who pinned their code in the first comment sold noticeably more
- Mid-tier TikTok reviewers outperformed larger Instagram accounts for this product

## Recommendations (scale / keep / stop)
- **Scale:** @theserumdiary and @glowroutine.co — offer paid posts next round; they have proven they can sell, not just post
- **Keep:** @skinbysana for content creation (UGC) — her audience trusts her, and her videos are reusable as brand content
- **Stop (affiliate):** @morningglowclub — keep friendly relations, but her audience does not buy through codes
- **Follow up:** @dermtalkdaily's long-form review could outperform everything here over time; keep the relationship warm
